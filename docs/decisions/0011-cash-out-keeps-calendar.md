# 0011: Running out of cash does not rewind time

**Context.** The brief says cash-out triggers a post-mortem, then restart, pivot or exit.

**Decision.** The semester calendar keeps moving. After the post-mortem, the group picks:
- **Restart**: same venture, continues from the next period. New capital must be raised through the financing process (a grant, family or a loan, with its wait and possible rejection). Outstanding debt is **not** cleared.
- **Pivot**: a changed offer or segment (recorded with what changed, why, triggering evidence, expected outcome). Same rules for capital and debt.
- **Exit**: the venture stops; the group completes the autopsy and still reflects on the remaining periods' market.

**Consequences.** Failing early costs periods, which is the real cost of failure, but never ends the course. Debt survives restarts, preserving the "debt never disappears" invariant.
